Getting started

Quickstart

Go from an empty workspace to your first invoice in a few minutes.

1. Invite your team

Add teammates from Settings → Users, or send bulk invitations by email. Give admin access to anyone who should manage modules, layouts, and permissions.

EmailRoleStatus
maria@northwind.comAdminAccepted

2. Add your first records

Start with Leads and Accounts — add them by hand, or ask us about migrating data from your current CRM. Every record supports custom fields alongside the built-in ones.

Leads Accounts Contacts Deals + your own module

3. Customize your layout

Open Settings → Modules → Layouts to reorder fields, mark them required or read-only, and choose what shows on the list, record, related, and PDF views. Every layout is stored as plain JSON under the hood:

{
  "fields": [
    { "name": "amount", "required": true },
    { "name": "sales_stage", "sortable": true }
  ]
}

4. Send your first quote or invoice

Add line items, and Cubrel calculates subtotals, discounts, and tax automatically. Generate a branded PDF from your default template in one click.

subtotal         = unit_price × quantity
discount_amount = subtotal × (discount / 100)
tax_amount        = (subtotal − discount_amount) × (tax_rate / 100)
total          = subtotal − discount_amount + tax_amount
💡

Next: build a custom module for the record type unique to your business, or design a dashboard that surfaces what your team checks every morning.
