# Setting Your Preferences in Cubrel

In this article we cover the difference between your Profile and your Preferences, what you can configure, and how personal overrides relate to your organization's defaults.

## Profile vs. Preferences

**Profile** is you as a person: name, username, email, phone, title, and avatar, the same kind of information any other record in Cubrel holds. **Preferences** is about how the app behaves for you: language, formatting, colors, list sizes, and notifications. They're two separate pages for two different kinds of settings.

## What you can configure

Preferences is organized into a few groups:

- **General** — interface language, date format, and date/time format.
- **Style** — your primary, secondary, success, and danger colors, plus a toggle to use each module's own color instead of your chosen ones.
- **Lists** — how many rows show at once in related-record panels, list views, and linking panels.
- **Notifications** — for each type of event (a record assigned to you, a meeting invite, a task due soon, an invite accepted or expired, activity on a record, being impersonated, a record converted, or a transformation triggered), separate toggles for whether you get an email, an in-app notification, or both.

## System defaults and personal overrides

Every preference has an organization-wide default set by an admin. When you haven't changed a setting yourself, you're automatically using that default, and if an admin updates it later, you'll follow along automatically. The moment you change a setting yourself, it becomes a personal override, from then on it stays fixed to your choice even if the organization's default changes later.

Any field you've overridden shows a small reset button next to it, click it to drop your override and go back to following the organization's default again.

## Saving changes

Changes to Preferences aren't applied as you type, they're staged until you click **Save**. If you change your mind before saving, use the discard/reset option to revert everything back to what it was when you opened the page.

## In short

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Where do I change my name or email? | **Profile**, not Preferences |
| Where do I change language, colors, or notifications? | **Preferences** |
| What happens if I never touch a preference? | It follows your organization's default automatically |
| What happens once I change one myself? | It becomes a personal override, fixed until you reset it |
| How do I go back to the organization's default? | Click the reset button next to that field |
| Do changes apply immediately? | No, only after clicking **Save** |
